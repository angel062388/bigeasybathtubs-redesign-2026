The Value of Walk-in Bathtub Refinishing

We aim to provide our clients with the best possible service so they can experience the full value of bathtub refinishing. Trying to decide whether you should refinish or replace your walk-in bathtub? We’ll dig into the benefits of a well-done bathtub refinish below.

  1. 01

    It’s Cheaper

    Why spend more on getting the latest bathtub if there’s a way to revamp your old one? Cosmetic issues don’t make a bathtub unusable — you shouldn’t jump to replace it as soon as it shows wear. The best way to remedy any cosmetic issue, such as scratches or stains, is with a good, old-fashioned refinish from a reliable contractor.

    While there are walk-in bathtub options that aren’t that expensive, you also need to consider the other expenses that come with tub replacement. You need to have the old tub removed, get another installed, and shell out some serious money to update the tiling and plumbing. When compared to the price of bathtub refinishing or bathtub resurfacing, you may be shocked by the difference.

  2. 02

    It’s Faster

    Since a lot of things need to be accomplished to complete a bathtub replacement, your contractor may need several days to complete the entire project. Not only does this contribute to the hefty price of bathtub replacement, but it also means that you can’t use your bathtub for that period of time.

    This makes bathtub refinishing the go-to choice for a lot of homeowners. This project can typically be completed in only three to six hours. Once the contractor leaves, you only need to wait about a day for the materials to cure properly. After that, you can use the bathtub again as you normally would.

  3. 03

    It’s Simpler for Homeowners

    If you like the design of your current bathtub and you can’t find another that looks just like it, refinishing your walk-in tub is the perfect solution. This can be especially useful for homes with unique or classic bathtubs that were installed decades ago; with bathtub refinishing, you can restore your bathtub’s former beauty and preserve your bathroom’s original style.

    By hiring a reliable contractor to carry out the refinishing project, you can effectively prolong the lifespan of your walk-in bathtub for about 10 – 15 more years. This makes it a very cost-effective solution; it should be your go-to option for any bathtub in your home that has significant scratches and chips.

  4. 04

    It’s Less Wasteful

    When it comes to bathroom improvement, it’s not always about upgrading your fixtures. Sometimes, you can simply refurbish them in order to make them look brand-new once again.

    By reglazing your bathtub, you’ll be able to save it and prevent it from ending in a landfill. If you’re looking for more sustainable solutions, bathtub refinishing is the way to go. That way, you can reduce the amount of waste you produce and minimize your carbon footprint.

01How long does a bathtub installation take?

Most standard bathtub installations are completed in one to two days. The timeline can vary with the type of tub, any plumbing changes, and the condition of your existing bathroom. We'll give you a clear estimate before we begin.

02Do you install walk-in tubs for seniors and accessibility?

Yes. Walk-in tubs are one of our specialties. We help you choose a safe, comfortable model with features like a low step-in threshold, built-in seating and grab bars, then handle the complete installation.

03What is bathtub refinishing, and how long does it last?

Refinishing restores the surface of your existing tub. We repair chips and stains and apply a fresh, durable coating for a like-new look at a fraction of the cost of replacement. With proper care, a quality refinish typically lasts 10 to 15 years.

04Should I repair, refinish, or replace my bathtub?

It depends on the tub's condition and your goals. Minor cracks, stains or a dated color are often perfect for repair or refinishing. Structural damage, leaks or a full layout change usually call for replacement. We'll assess it honestly during your free estimate.

05Do you restore antique clawfoot tubs?

Absolutely. We refinish and restore clawfoot and other vintage tubs, inside and out, bringing back their classic charm while making them fully functional for everyday use.
