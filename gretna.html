About Gretna

Getting to know Gretna, Louisiana

Gretna is the seat of Jefferson Parish, on the west bank of the Mississippi River across from uptown New Orleans.

  1. Things to Do

    Come for the Gretna Heritage Festival, or visit the German American Cultural Center, which hosts Maifest and Oktoberfest.

  2. Nearby Suburbs

    Gretna is part of the New Orleans, Metairie and Kenner metro area. Our team also works in Kenner and across the river in New Orleans.

  3. Fun Facts

    The town was settled in 1836 under the name Mechanikham and incorporated in 1913. Zatarain’s has been based here since 1963.

  4. Public Transportation

    The Crescent City Connection bridge crosses the Mississippi here. Jefferson Parish runs the Jefferson Transit bus system.
