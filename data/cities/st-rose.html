Refinish vs Replace

Our bathtub services use the best technology and innovation to deliver premium quality bathtub installation, refinish, and repair services. We are bath specialists and we can help you recreate your bathroom for an ultimate bath experience for years to come.

When your bathtub shows signs of aging or damage, we can refinish or replace your tub. The decision is for you to make but we can help you make the best decision. Depending on the condition of your old tub, both a refinish or replacement can be costly.

With a few issues, a refinish can be more economical and wise because it can be dealt with only quick fixes. However, if your bathtub has reached the end of its life, it can be best to replace it because repairing can only be a short-term remedy and you will need to pay for frequent repairs in the future.

Here are questions that would help you decide if you need a tub replacement or refinish:

  1. Can your tub be easily uninstalled?
  2. What is your tub made of? Does it have any visible or questionable damage?
  3. Are you planning to sell or remodel your home soon?
  4. How is the tile surrounding your tub?
  5. What’s your budget?

Our bathtub installation contractor can help you know more about bathtub replacement and refinishing services so you can make a well-informed decision.

When Should a Tub Replacement Be a Good Option?

All bathtubs will reach the end of their life. When your bathtub reaches at least 10-30 years, or 50 years maximum, depending on the materials it is made of, you can say that it is best to replace it rather than have it repaired or refinished.
