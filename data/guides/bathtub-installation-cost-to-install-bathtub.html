Different Bathtub Types and Their Costs

One of the biggest factors that affect the cost to install a tub is the type of tub being installed. Generally speaking, there are two types of tubs available—freestanding and built-in.

Freestanding tubs are typically more expensive than built-ins because they require additional framing and a more complex installation process. Additionally, freestanding tubs usually come in larger sizes compared to built-ins, which can also increase the overall cost.

Built-in tubs are a great option if you’re looking for an economical yet stylish way to upgrade your bathroom. These tubs come in a variety of shapes and sizes, allowing you to find the perfect fit for your space. Additionally, these tubs are typically much easier to install than freestanding tubs, which can significantly lower the cost of installation.

If you choose an acrylic or cast iron model, expect to pay more than with other materials like fiberglass or plastic.  Cast iron tubs tend to be pricier since they’re heavier and require extra care during installation.

The average cost of installing a bathtub according to the type of tub you choose is listed below:

  • All-purpose tub: $200 – $800
  • Freestanding/clawfoot tub: $750 – $12,000
  • Jet/Whirlpool tub: $1,500 – $14,000
  • Soaking tub: $600 – $13,000
  • Walk-in tub: $2,000 – $8,000

Cost by Bathtub Material

Choosing the right bathtub material is an often overlooked but important decision. Not only does bathtub material affect the aesthetic appeal of your bathroom, but it also affects cost. Popular bathtub materials include durable porcelain, high-end acrylics, and energy-efficient fiberglass.

The average cost of installation according to the material used is listed below:

  • Acrylic: $200 – $14,000
  • Cast Iron: $500 – $10,000
  • Copper: $2,000 – $10,000
  • Enameled Steel: $200 – $2,500
  • Fiberglass: $200 – $4,500
  • Granite: $3,000 – $20,000
  • Marble: $1,400 – $10,000
  • Solid Surface: $2,000 – $6,000

Bathtub Surround Costs

Installing a bathtub surround can be an inexpensive and effective way to give your bath a fresh, modern look. The cost of bathtub surrounds varies by style, size, and material, but generally ranges from around $100-$400.

  • Low-cost bathtub surrounds are made of easy-to-install plastic panels that can be self-installed in a single day.
  • Mid-range bathtub surrounds are frequently composed of durable acrylic materials and provide varied styles, colors, and patterns.
  • Higher-end bathtub surrounds tend to use more stylish natural materials such as stone or marble mosaic tiles that result in a luxurious finish.

Additional Features & Accessories

If you want to add additional features or accessories such as jets or lighting fixtures, this will also affect the overall cost of installing your new tub. Depending on what kind of additions you choose and where they’re located (e.g., built into the walls), these extras can add hundreds or even thousands of dollars to the final bill. It’s important to factor in any upgrades before committing to an installation so that you know exactly how much money needs to be set aside for this project!

Tips for Installing a Bathtub

Installing a bathtub can be a daunting task, but with the right bathtub installation tips, it can be much easier.

  1. To begin, choose the bathtub you want to install carefully – considering its size and type – to avoid any issues you may have in the installation process. Professional help is recommended if you are having trouble understanding anything or working with awkward angles.
  2. Measure out your bathtub space so that you know where it should go and can check for any issues like pipes or wiring. Then make sure the surface underneath is flat and even to avoid any problems when placing down your bathtub fixtures.

Having done so, it’s now time to place your bathtub into position, doing all necessary safety checks while at it before fixing down and finalizing your bathtub!
