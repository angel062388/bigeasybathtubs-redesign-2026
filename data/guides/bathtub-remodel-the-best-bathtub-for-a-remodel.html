Best Bathtub Styles For Your Bathroom Remodel in New Orleans

For a bathroom remodel in New Orleans, there are a variety of bathtub styles to choose from. From classic freestanding clawfoot tubs and air bathtubs to whirlpool tubs and modern soaking tubs, there is something for every budget and home style.

Freestanding Tubs

Freestanding tubs are one of the most popular types of bathtubs for bathroom remodels in New Orleans. They create a great focal point in the space and offer people the option to choose between deep soaking baths or quick showers. Freestanding tubs can be made from almost any material, including acrylic, stone, metal, or wood.

Furthermore, since these units don’t rely on walls for mounting, they are much easier to install than traditional built-in bathtubs. That makes them perfect for bathrooms with limited space or for those looking for a unique look when it comes to their bathroom remodel in New Orleans.

Air Tubs

Air tubs are one of the best bathtub styles for bathroom remodeling in New Orleans as they provide a luxurious and spa-like experience. An air tub generates thousands of tiny hydromassage bubbles to create a relaxing massage and calming atmosphere; it also reinforces heat retention which keeps water warm longer than traditional bathtubs and is energy efficient.

Air tubs come in various shapes and sizes making them easier to fit in your bathroom while ensuring the perfect fit. They are also quite hygienic due to their rounded features which makes them easy to clean and maintain. Overall, air tubs offer great style, and luxurious comfort along with numerous benefits for your bathroom remodel in New Orleans.

Round Bathtub

Round bathtubs are an ideal choice for a bathroom remodel in New Orleans. Whether you’re looking for something simple and timeless or bold and dramatic, round bathtubs offer the perfect match. Choose from a variety of materials like acrylic, stone, enameled cast iron, or copper to get just the right look in your space.

The round designs can also fit into smaller bathrooms and areas, making them a practical yet fashionable option for any home. As long as you have three feet of open space, these tubs can be installed easily with standard plumbing fixtures. Your bathroom remodel project is sure to be stylish and successful if you choose one of New Orleans’ best styles – a round bathtub!

Traditional Clawfoot Bathtubs

Traditional clawfoot bathtubs are a classic timeless style for your bathroom. They offer modern convenience with a vintage feel that is ideal for New Orleans, an area known for its French-Creole and Southern charm. Clawfoot bathtubs can be constructed from a range of materials such as cast iron, acrylic, or copper. They also come in various shapes and sizes to customize your bathroom remodel in order to suit your lifestyle.

Alcove or Corner Bathtubs

For bathroom remodels in New Orleans, Alcove or Corner Bathtubs are the best bathtub styles. Not only do they provide a luxurious, spacious look to a bathroom, but they also offer great functionality, with corner alcoves providing plenty of storage and seating space.

How Much Should You Spend On A Bathtub in New Orleans?

The cost of a bathtub in New Orleans can vary greatly depending on the type, quality, and style of bathtub you choose. Generally speaking, inexpensive bathtubs can cost around $100-$200 and midrange options can range from $400 – $600.

High-end luxury tubs may cost more than $1,000 or even higher. Factors such as size and special features like jets can also affect the price significantly. Labor costs for installation should also be considered when budgeting for a bathtub in New Orleans.
