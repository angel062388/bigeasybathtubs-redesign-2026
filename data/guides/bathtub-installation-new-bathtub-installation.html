Types of Bathtubs

There are several types of bathtubs available in the market today. The most common type is the alcove bathtub, which is designed to fit between three walls. Another popular type is the freestanding bathtub, which sits on four legs. Corner bathtubs are also available, which are designed to fit in the corner of the bathroom.

Drop-in bathtubs are those that are mounted on a platform, while under-mount bathtubs are installed underneath the platform. Clawfoot bathtubs, which are freestanding and feature ornate legs, are a classic choice for people who love vintage aesthetics.

Whirlpool bathtubs, with built-in jets, can provide a soothing spa experience while soaking. The choice of bathtub type depends on personal preferences, bathroom layout, and budget.

Bathtub Materials

A new bathtub installation involves consideration of different types of bathtub materials. Bathtub materials vary in terms of their durability, maintenance, and cost. The most commonly used materials include acrylic, porcelain, fiberglass, and cast iron. Acrylic bathtubs are popular because they are lightweight, easy to install, and come in various sizes and colors. Porcelain bathtubs are durable, and have a classic look, but they are generally expensive to install and require careful maintenance.

Bathtub Size and Shape

When it comes to new bathtub installation, the size and shape of the bathtub are of utmost importance. The size of the bathtub should be chosen based on the available space in the bathroom and the needs of the user. If the bathroom has limited space, a smaller-sized bathtub would be more appropriate.

The shape of the bathtub is also important as it can affect the comfort level of the user. In general, bathtubs come in rectangular, square, oval, and circular shapes. The shape of the bathtub should be chosen based on the personal preferences of the user, as well as the overall design of the bathroom. Ultimately, selecting the right size and shape of the bathtub will ensure a comfortable and enjoyable bathing experience.

Bathtub Installation Process

The bathtub installation process involves several steps to ensure a proper and safe installation.

  • First, the old bathtub and any surrounding materials are removed.
  • Next, the area is prepared for the new bathtub, including making any necessary adjustments to the plumbing and ensuring the floor is level.
  • The new bathtub is then set in place and secured to the surrounding walls and floor.
  • Finally, any caulking and sealing are done to prevent water leakage.

It’s important to have a professional handle this process to avoid any potential issues or mistakes that could cause damage or risk to the home or its occupants.

Bathtub Accessories

While new bathtub installations can include bathtub accessories, it is not necessarily synonymous with them. Bathtub accessories, such as grab bars, bath mats, and shower caddies, can be added to an existing bathtub or shower to enhance safety, comfort, and organization.

However, when a new bathtub is installed, the focus is primarily on the bathtub unit itself, including the material, style, size, and placement. Once the new bathtub is in place, accessories can be added as desired to further improve the bathing experience.
