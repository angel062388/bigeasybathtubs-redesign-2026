5. Safety Precautions

When it comes to installing a bathtub, safety should never be compromised – take measures beforehand to ensure everyone’s well-being. Put on protective equipment such as clothing and eyewear; additionally, handle tools with extreme caution and keep materials away from areas where they could cause injury or damage.

Taking these precautions will help guarantee that the installation process goes smoothly without any hiccups along the way!

Additionally, it is important to make sure that all electrical connections are properly grounded and that any gas lines are properly sealed. Finally, be sure to follow all instructions provided in the instruction manual to ensure a safe and successful installation.

6. Tools Needed for Installation

To successfully install your bathtub with our installation kit, you will need several essential tools: a drill, screwdriver, adjustable wrench, level, and tape measure. Depending on the type of tub you are installing though, there may be other pieces of equipment necessary as well.

For example, if you are installing a drop-in tub, you will need to have access to a jigsaw or reciprocating saw.

7. Care and Maintenance Guide

Proper care and maintenance of your bathtub are essential to ensure its longevity. To keep your bathtub looking its best, it is important to clean it regularly with mild soap and warm water.

Furthermore, make sure to thoroughly examine the tub for any wear and tear like chips or cracks. If noticed, it is important to repair them as soon as possible in order to prevent further damage from occurring.

Lastly, always check all plumbing connections regularly for potential leaks or other complications that may arise.
