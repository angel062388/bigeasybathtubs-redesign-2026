The Cost-Benefit Analysis of Refinishing vs. Replacing Your Bathtub: Is It Cheaper to Refinish or Replace Your Tub?

When faced with the decision of whether to refinish or replace your bathtub, it can be difficult to weigh up all the factors. We will explore some of the major considerations you should take into account when making this decision.

Cost Comparison of Refinishing vs. Replacing a Tub

The cost of refinishing a tub is typically less than the cost of replacing a tub. However, the cost will depend on several factors such as the condition of the existing tub, the materials and labor costs in your area, and whether you hire a professional or do the work yourself.

Refinishing

The average cost of refinishing a bathtub is between $400 to $1,200. It is a process of repairing and reglazing the surface of a bathtub. It can be done to fix minor damage, such as chips and scratches, and can also be used to change the color of the tub. In addition, refinishing can make the bathtub look new again and make it more resistant to future damage.

Replacing

Replacing a bathtub can cost between $1,500 to $5,000 or more, depending on the size of the tub, the materials used, and the labor costs in your area. This process is more invasive than refinishing, as it involves removing the existing tub and installing a new one. This option may be the best choice if the tub is in poor condition, if it is an outdated style or color, or if the size or layout of the tub needs to be changed.

So, overall refinishing can be more affordable than replacing a bathtub, but it all depends on the state of the existing tub, the goals you have for your bathroom, and also on your budget.

Cost Factors in Replacing vs Refinishing a Bathtub

Several cost factors come into play when comparing the costs of replacing a bathtub versus refinishing a bathtub.

  1. Materials: The cost of the new bathtub and any additional materials required for installation will be a significant factor when replacing a bathtub. While refinishing typically only requires materials for repair and reglazing the surface of the tub.
  2. Labor: Replacing a bathtub is more labor-intensive than refinishing a bathtub, which requires removing and disposing of the existing tub, as well as installing the new one. This can drive up labor costs. Refinishing a bathtub typically only requires the surface to be prepared, repaired, and re-glazed.
  3. Location: Labor and materials costs can vary widely depending on where you live, so the cost of a project in one location may be significantly higher or lower than in another location.
  4. Condition of the Tub: If your tub is in poor condition, replacing might be the only option, and it will be more expensive than if the tub is in good condition and only need refinishing.
  5. Size of the Tub: The cost of replacing a tub may be affected by the size of the tub, as larger tubs will require more materials and labor.
  6. Additional work: If you’re replacing the tub and you are also remodeling the bathroom, additional work will be required and that will affect the final cost.

It is important to consider all of these factors and get detailed estimates from professionals before making a decision. Keep in mind that refinishing a bathtub may not be able to correct significant damage or structural issues, but is a cost-effective way to update the look of your bathroom and extend the life of your tub.

Pros and Cons of Refinishing vs Replacing a Tub

Refinishing a tub has some advantages and disadvantages compared to replacing a tub.

Pros of Refinishing a Tub

  • It is generally less expensive than replacing a tub.
  • It is less invasive, as it only requires the repair and reglazing of the surface of the tub, rather than the removal and installation of a new tub.
  • It can be completed in a shorter amount of time than replacing a tub.
  • It can be used to fix minor damage, such as chips and scratches, and can also be used to change the color of the tub.
  • It can make the bathtub look new again and make it more resistant to future damage.

Cons of Refinishing a Tub

  • The finish on a refinished tub may not be as durable as a new tub and may require more frequent maintenance.
  • It may not be able to correct significant damage or structural issues.
  • It may not be able to change the size or layout of the tub.
  • The finish on a refinished tub may not be as smooth and even as on a new tub, and may show imperfections.

Pros of Replacing a Tub

  • If the tub is in poor condition, replacing is the best option to ensure the bathtub is safe and functional.
  • It provides a fresh new look and feels to the bathroom.
  • allows choosing a new style, size, and color, to match the remodeled bathroom.
  • It can correct structural issues that cannot be fixed by refinishing.

Cons of Replacing a Tub

  • It is more expensive than refinishing, as it involves removing and installing a new tub.
  • It is more invasive and will take longer to complete.
  • It is a more disruptive process and may require temporarily relocating out of the bathroom while the work is being done.

As you can see both options have their advantages and disadvantages, it all depends on the condition of the existing tub, the goals you have for your bathroom, and your budget. It’s important to evaluate your options and consider the long-term costs and benefits of each before making a decision.
