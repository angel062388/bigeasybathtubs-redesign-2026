How To Make Sure A Bathroom is Designed Safe For Seniors?

When designing a bathroom for seniors, safety should be the top priority. First, the flooring needs to have non-slip features such as textured tiles or rubber treads that lead to the bathtub and shower area.

  • Add Grab Bars

Installing grab bars in a bathroom designed for seniors is essential to making sure the space is accessible and safe. Grab bars are a specific type of bar that can be mounted near a toilet, shower, bathtub, or other surfaces in the bathroom.

  • Choose Flat Surfaces With Good Grip

When designing a bathroom for seniors, making sure it is safe and functional should be the top priority. One way to do this is to choose flat surfaces that provide a good grip. Bathroom floors should be non-slip in order to decrease the risk of falls.

  • Install a Walk-in Shower or Tub With a Non-skid Bottom and Side Rails

Installing a walk-in shower or tub with a non-skid bottom and side rails is essential for designing a bathroom that is safe for seniors. Non-skid flooring can prevent falls, which are the most common injuries found in baths.

  • Place the Toilet Higher Than Usual So That It Is Easier to Get Up and Down

It is important to design a senior-friendly bathroom in order to maintain safety and reduce the risk of falls. The toilet should be placed higher than usual, as it would be easier for seniors to get up and down when using it. An armrest can also be included beside the toilet for added support.

  • Use a Chair With Arms to Help Them Sit Down and Stand Up Safely

Using a chair with arms in the design of senior-friendly bathrooms can help seniors safely sit down and stand up. When planning the design of the bathroom, consider chairs or benches with armrests to add support when getting on and off the toilet or stepping into the bathtub. This type of seating will also add stability for those who may be unsteady on their feet.

Also, make sure there is sufficient lighting around toilets and tubs to ensure that seniors can see clearly while they use the facilities. Finally, it is important to choose slip-resistant floors around the toilet and shower area to prevent falls while getting in and out of the bathtub.
