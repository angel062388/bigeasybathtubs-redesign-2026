Bath Tub Remodeling vs Tub Replacement

One of the best ways to remodel a bathroom in New Orleans is either a bathtub remodel or a tub replacement.  Here’s the difference between the two:

Bathtub Remodeling

The processes involved in bathtub remodeling include resurfacing, re-coating, refinishing, or re-glazing.  We remodel bathtubs by filling in cracks and worn-out areas and applying several coats of primer and paint before applying a final buff.

This bathroom remodeling service works best for bathtubs that are in pretty good shape.  If your tub is covered with minor stains, scratches, and other surface imperfections, bathtub remodeling will be a great option.

Tub Replacement

Make a fresh start by replacing your old and worn-out bathtub with a new tub.  This bathroom remodel can be quite expensive but it can be cost-effective in the long run if your bathtub has gone through several remodeling or re-glazing already.

The process of tub replacement involves removing the tile wall surround and installing your choice of the wall surround like a tile wall surround, acrylic wall surround, stone wall surround, natural stone wall, and the like.

You can customize your bathroom with a tub replacement service from the bath experts.  We can create an entirely new bathroom space in your New Orleans home for you.  So if you are tired or bored of the shape, size, or location of your existing tub, call us for a bath remodel service.
