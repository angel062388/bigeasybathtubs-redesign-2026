How Is Bathtub Repair Done?

Bathtub repair is not as difficult as it may seem, but it must be done correctly to avoid further damage. Most bathtub repair or refinishing is minor and can be easily fixed by the homeowner. However, there are some cases where the damage is too big, and professionals will need to be called to handle the project.

Here’s how bathtub repair is typically done:

1. Assessment

To know what needs to be repaired, a proper assessment must be done. This will determine the severity of the damage and what steps need to take place for bathroom tub repair.

2. Cleaning

The next step is to clean the area around the bathtub and the tub itself to ensure that no dirt or debris will get in the way of the repair process.

3. Repairing

Once the area is clean, the actual repair or refinishing can begin. For small cracks or chips, a simple patch kit will do the trick. For more significant damage, however, professional help is necessary.

4. Sealing

To finish up, the porcelain chip repair area will need to be sealed. This will protect the repair porcelain chip and make sure that it lasts for a long time to come so you can avoid further bathtub damage. With this, our share of the maintenance is complete!

How Much Does it Cost to Repair a Bathtub?

The cost of repairing a bathtub can vary depending on the severity of the damage.

Several factors will affect the price, such as:

  • The material the tub is made of: Depending on the material, tub repair costs will differ. It is essential to know what your tub is made of so you can ask for an accurate repair quote. If you don’t know, let us know and we will figure it out for you.
  • Size of the tub: Larger tubs will be more expensive to repair than smaller ones because they require more work and materials. Heavier tubs may also need special equipment to be lifted during the repair process, which will add to the cost.
  • The extent of the damage: The more damage there is, the more it will cost to repair. If there are just a few cracks or chips, the repair will be less expensive than if the tub is severely damaged or needs to be replaced.

Is it Cheaper to Replace My Tub or Repair It?

Just like any home appliance, a bathtub will eventually need some repair or maintenance. Depending on the severity of the damage and how old your tub is, it might be cheaper to consider replacement of your old bathtub than to repair it.

But if you are going to have your tub checked by a professional, you might as well have them fix it if the repair is less expensive than having to replace the whole tub.

What Tubs Are Repairable?

Not all tubs are repairable; some are simply too old or too damaged. Also, if your tub is made of a material that cannot be repaired, then it will need to be replaced.

That’s why it’s important to consult with a professional bathtub repair service to assess the damage so you will be given the best options.

To have a general idea, porcelain tubs, acrylic tubs, fiberglass tubs, and cultured marble tubs are repairable. With that being said, most tubs can be fixed as long as the damage is not too severe.
