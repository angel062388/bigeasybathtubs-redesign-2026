Replacement Bathtubs Cost in New Orleans

The average price range to replace a bathtub in New Orleans area is $1,300 to $10,000, while the national average for one new tub is $5,600.

The price variation is primarily due to the bathtub you choose, the contractor or bath fitter you hire, the current condition of your bathroom, areas that need repair, labor and materials, and other factors.

If you choose to replace more than one tub, your costs will rise, though you may be able to save some money by having your bath fitter installed on the same day.

Types of Bathtubs

  • Alcove or Recessed Bathtubs: Tub that is built into a wall and is surrounded on three sides.
  • Tub and Shower: A tub and shower combo where the shower head sprays into the bathtub.
  • Corner Tubs: A corner tub is usually separate from the shower, with a single finished frontal side and two unfinished sides against the walls.
  • Freestanding Tubs: Finished on all sides and features a variety of designs and decorations
  • Walk-In Bathtubs: Perfect for elders and others who have trouble getting in and out of other bathtubs.
  • Garden Tubs: Luxurious, huge, standalone soaking tubs, frequently a bathroom standout piece.
