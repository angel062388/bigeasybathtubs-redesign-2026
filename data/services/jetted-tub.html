What Are the Features of a Jetted Tub

Jetted tubs are an increasingly popular feature in modern bathrooms. These luxurious tubs offer a number of features that set them apart from other bathtubs. Jetted tubs typically come with adjustable water pressure, allowing you to enjoy a relaxing massage. They also come with a powerful internal cleansing system, typically featuring many adjustable jets to allow you to concentrate the water pressure where it is most needed and desired.

Water Jets

Jetted tubs are popular because of the relaxing features that they offer. They feature water jets that use air or a pump to circulate water in the tub. These can be used to massage the neck, shoulders, and other body parts, relieving tension and helping to improve circulation.

Pump

A jetted tub is a type of bathtub that uses pumps to create pressure and bubbles in the water. It consists of several jets strategically placed throughout the bathtub, and an air filter to keep the water clean. In addition, jetted tubs often feature a light therapy package with adjustable LED colors for a more soothing experience.

Water Control

Jetted tubs feature adjustable water control systems that allow you to customize the speed, pressure, and temperature of the water for maximum comfort. You can also easily control the number of air jets used to produce bubbles and massage the body with just a few simple switches.

Types of Jetted Tubs

Jetted tubs are an increasingly popular choice thanks to their luxurious and spa-like benefits. They come in a variety of shapes and sizes including round, rectangular, and corner models, giving consumers options for any existing bathroom layout.

Drop-in

Drop-in jetted tubs are stand-alone bathtubs that are installed in a raised platform specifically made to fit the tub, and are sometimes referred to as “deck mount” or “deck mounted” jetted tubs. The raised platform provides stability and support for the weight of the water and the person inside.

Corner

Corner jetted tubs are the perfect choice for those who have smaller bathrooms. A corner tub saves space while providing a luxurious spa experience with powerful massage jets and/or hydrotherapy. There are many types of corner jetted tubs, including freestanding, drop-in, tile-in, and alcove models, in shapes ranging from hexagons and rectangles to triangular designs.

Clawfoot

Clawfoot jetted tubs are an ideal choice for people looking for a luxurious and timeless look in their bathroom. They feature an elevated, freestanding design typically made of cast iron or acrylic, and come in classic clawfoot shapes that make it easy to accessorize with the latest trends. Clawfoot jetted tubs also offer various massage jets that provide users with an invigorating aquatic massage experience.

Walk-in

Walk-in jetted tubs are one of the most popular types of jetted tubs, and they are easy to install. These tubs provide a safe and comfortable experience while bathing, as they are equipped with an extra wide doorway that can walk in or stand up. This type of tub also has more jets than traditional bathtubs, creating greater water pressure for a more invigorating massage.

What Are the Costs of a Jetted Tub?

The cost of a jetted tub varies depending on the type you choose and its size. Entry-level jetted tubs can range from a few thousand dollars, up to tens of thousands. Luxury models are even more expensive due to high-end features such as Bluetooth connectivity and chromotherapy lighting.

Installation costs for a jetted tub are typically between $500 and $2,000, depending on the complexity of the project. In addition to the initial purchase and installation price, there are ongoing operational costs associated with the upkeep of a jetted tub, such as increased energy use for heating the water and running motors that power the jets.

The best way to estimate your total cost is to consult local contractors who specialize in hot tub installation.
