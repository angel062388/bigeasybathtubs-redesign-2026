Reasons for Bathtub Refinishing or Resurfacing

Keep in mind that bathtub refinishing is a cosmetic repair. This means refinishing, which is also known as reglazing or recoating, aims to get rid of any imperfection on your tub.

Here’s how professionals like us can accomplish such work:

  • Our professional refinishing expert will begin by sanding the tub’s surface. Doing so will remove any light stains or scratches.
  • Once stains or scratches are reduced, our refinishers will fill any cracks or chips.
  • After filling cracks, the refinishing professional will then cover the tub with paint and primer. Depending on your tub’s condition, our contractor may add multiple coatings.
  • After painting, the entire process will end with one last buffing.

Now that you know the general process, here are some signs that indicate you need a refinishing expert for your home.
