Benefits of Bathtub Wall Surrounds

Better Options

Blank, generic white wall coverings may be the first thing that comes to mind when you think of wall surrounds. But there are actually numerous varieties of color, texture, and even patterns for these covers. For instance, you can use a colored wall surround to bring extra radiance to your bathroom or a textured one to resemble expensive stone or tile without spending much. If you want an even more unique style, go for a patterned wall surround that looks like mosaic tiling.

Options Are Cost-Effective

Installing bathroom tile is usually much more costly than wall surround because, on top of the cost of the tiles themselves, you must also pay for skilled labor. The expense of a wall surround will vary depending on the texture and color you decide on, yet they are simpler to move and install, which lessens the cost.

Moreover, installing a wall surround is significantly less complex than tile work as we will discuss in section five; this reduces labor costs as well. For example, putting up a wall surround with a stone-like or tiled texture can present your restroom with an upscale look at a minimal amount; wisely, establishing a patterned wall surround could give off an effect that looks similar to mosaic tile.

Easy Installation

Wall surrounds are seen as sensible for those renovating their single bathroom or those needing to quickly sell their house since installing walls is simpler than putting tiles on a washroom wall. Not only does this straightforward installation minimize labor expenses, but it also dwindles the length of the renovation. Therefore, putting up wall surrounds is more advantageous than applying tiles for individuals in these situations.

Waterproof

Since wall surrounds have fewer gaps than tiling, they tend to be more watertight for longer periods. Properly installed wall surrounds can last as long as the shower is up and running. This better waterproofing lessens the chance of a build-up of mold below the covering of the walls, plus it decreases the possibility of rotting that could harm walls or flooring.

Smooth Appearance

Grout lines often make restroom walls seem tight and disorganized, which is especially true in more limited quarters since any interference to the view can further shrink the area. Wall surrounds provide a polished, smooth look that can enhance a bathroom’s spacing.

To boost this impact, you may be able to select a wall surround that continues all the way up to the roof, making the space appear taller. It doesn’t matter how large or small your bathroom is – a consistent wall surround can make an impressive statement.

How Much Do Tub Surrounds Cost?

The cost of a tub surround will depend on the type and size of the surround you choose. Generally, basic wall surrounds can range from $200 to $400 for a standard-sized tub. However, if you opt for more intricate designs or materials such as stone or tile, the cost can be much higher.

Additionally, installation costs should also be taken into consideration when budgeting for your new bathtub wall surround. Depending on the complexity of the job, labor costs can range from $200 to $800 or more.

It is important to note that these estimates are just that – estimates – and may vary depending on the size of your bathroom and other factors. It is best to consult with a professional contractor in order to get an accurate estimate of the cost of your project.
