Can a walk-in bathtub be repaired?

A walk-in bathtub is a great addition to any bathroom, providing a safe and convenient way to take a bath. But what happens if your walk-in bathtub starts to leak? Is it time to replace the old tub, or can it be repaired?

The good news is that most leaks in walk-in bathtubs can be repaired fairly easily. The first step is to identify the source of the leak. If the leak is coming from the drain, it may be due to a loose gasket or a cracked drain pipe. These problems can usually be fixed with a little silicone sealant or plumber’s putty.

If the leak is coming from the walls of the tub, it is likely due to a crack in the porcelain. Cracks in the porcelain can usually be repaired with epoxy putty or a similar product. Sometimes, the entire tub may need to be replaced. But if you have a small leak, there’s a good chance that it can be easily fixed.
