Bathtub Features

If you want the best bathing experience, consider various bathtub features, like the following:

1. Chromatherapy

Chromatherapy tubs include underwater LED lights. These will change color to complement your mood. For example, you have blue for a more soothing feel, pink for peace, and more.

2. Jets and Bubbles

Whirlpool tubs have various speed settings that control the jets and bubbles’ vigor. There are also in-line heaters to warm water while circulating, preventing heat loss. Silent motor technology helps decrease the jets’ sound. Then, foot massage jets are placed on top of submerged foot support.

3. Music

Some tubs include resonant speakers that play music and offer underwater vibrations on your skin to calm you during bath time. You can sync it with your playlists on the computer.

4. Digital Controls

You can do everything from adjusting the temperature and controlling the jet flow and direction to changing the color and playing selected music with a built-in touchpad.
