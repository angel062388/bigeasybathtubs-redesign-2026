01Are walk-in tubs hard to keep?

Walk-in tubs are becoming increasingly popular in New Orleans, as they offer a safe and convenient way to take a bath. Yet, some people worry that these tubs may be difficult to keep. In reality, walk-in tubs are no more difficult to care for than any other type of tub.

The key is to clean them regularly and to keep an eye out for any leaks or cracks. While it is important to take good care of your walk-in tub, you should not feel like you have to put extra effort into maintaining it.

With a bit of basic upkeep, your walk-in tub will last for many years. So go ahead and enjoy the convenience of your walk-in tub; there’s no need to worry about making it last.

02What are the problems with walk-in tubs?

Walk-in tubs are a type of senior bathing solution that has become increasingly popular in recent years. These tubs are designed to offer a safe and comfortable bathing experience for those with mobility issues.

But, walk-in tubs can pose a serious threat to public safety in New Orleans, LA. The problem is that walk-in tubs are often installed in homes without the proper plumbing infrastructure to support them.

As a result, these tubs can easily overflow, flooding the home and creating a slip-and-fall hazard. Also, walk-in tubs often need a lot of electrical work to install, which can create an electrical fire hazard if not done properly. As such, it is important to carefully consider the risks and benefits of walk-in tubs before installing one in your New Orleans home.

03Can a walk-in bathtub be repaired?

A walk-in bathtub is a great addition to any bathroom, providing a safe and convenient way to take a bath. But what happens if your walk-in bathtub starts to leak? Is it time to replace the old tub, or can it be repaired?

The good news is that most leaks in walk-in bathtubs can be repaired fairly easily. The first step is to identify the source of the leak. If the leak is coming from the drain, it may be due to a loose gasket or a cracked drain pipe. These problems can usually be fixed with a little silicone sealant or plumber’s putty.

If the leak is coming from the walls of the tub, it is likely due to a crack in the porcelain. Cracks in the porcelain can usually be repaired with epoxy putty or a similar product. Sometimes, the entire tub may need to be replaced. But if you have a small leak, there’s a good chance that it can be easily fixed.

04What keeps walk-in bathtubs from leaking?

When it comes to preventing leaks, a new tub has a few advantages over traditional tubs.

  1. First, walk-in tubs have a seal that helps keep water inside the tub.
  2. Second, they often have a built-in overflow drain that prevents water from spilling over the edge of the tub.
  3. Finally, many walk-in tubs are equipped with a door or curtain that creates a tight seal when closed, further preventing leaks. By taking these measures, walk-in tubs are able to provide a safer and more comfortable bathing experience for users.
05Do you install walk-in tubs for seniors and accessibility?

Yes. Walk-in tubs are one of our specialties. We help you choose a safe, comfortable model with features like a low step-in threshold, built-in seating and grab bars, then handle the complete installation.

06Should I repair, refinish, or replace my bathtub?

It depends on the tub's condition and your goals. Minor cracks, stains or a dated color are often perfect for repair or refinishing. Structural damage, leaks or a full layout change usually call for replacement. We'll assess it honestly during your free estimate.
