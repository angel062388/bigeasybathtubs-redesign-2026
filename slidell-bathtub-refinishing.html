Reasons for Bathtub Refinishing or Resurfacing in Slidell

Keep in mind that bathtub refinishing is a cosmetic repair. This means refinishing, which is also known as reglazing or recoating, aims to get rid of any imperfection on your tub.

Here’s how professionals like us can accomplish such work:

  • Our professional refinishing expert will begin by sanding the tub’s surface. Doing so will remove any light stains or scratches.
  • Once stains or scratches are reduced, our refinishers will fill any cracks or chips.
  • After filling cracks, the refinishing professional will then cover the tub with paint and primer. Depending on your tub’s condition, our contractor may add multiple coatings.
  • After painting, the entire process will end with one last buffing.

Now that you know the general process, here are some signs that indicate you need a refinishing expert for your home.

  1. 01

    No Budget for a New Bathtub

    Refinishing is far less expensive than purchasing a modern bathtub. So, if you’re tight on budget, consider taking the refinishing route rather than suffering from an old, worn-out tub.

  2. 02

    Keep Your Old, Expensive Bathtub

    If you have a claw-foot, antique, or cast-iron tub, replacing them can be extremely expensive. If your expensive tub has no significant damage, then refinishing it will make it aesthetically pleasing again without breaking the bank.

  3. 03

    A Simple Resurface Will Suffice

    The tub’s surface will have a major impact on the bathroom’s overall look. Hence, if your tub’s structure is still sturdy, yet it has minor stains, call us now so we can start with the refinishing process.

About Slidell

Getting to know Slidell, Louisiana

Slidell is in St. Tammany Parish, on the northeast shore of Lake Pontchartrain.

  1. Things to Do

    Browse the shops and restaurants of Olde Towne Slidell, visit the Slidell Museum, or launch a boat from Heritage Park.

  2. Nearby Suburbs

    Pearl River and Pearlington, Mississippi are close by. Our team also works in Mandeville and Covington.

  3. Fun Facts

    Slidell grew up in 1882 and 1883 around the building of the New Orleans and Northeastern Railroad. It is named for the politician John Slidell.

  4. Public Transportation

    Amtrak’s Crescent stops at Slidell station, and Interstates 10, 12 and 59 meet here. St. Tammany Parish runs STAR Transit, a curb-to-curb ride service booked by phone ahead of time.

01Do you offer bathtub refinishing in Slidell?

We serve New Orleans and the surrounding communities, including Baton Rouge, Covington, Mandeville, Slidell, Kenner, Gretna, Hammond, LaPlace, Madisonville, and St Rose.

02What is bathtub refinishing, and how long does it last?

Refinishing restores the surface of your existing tub. We repair chips and stains and apply a fresh, durable coating for a like-new look at a fraction of the cost of replacement. With proper care, a quality refinish typically lasts 10 to 15 years.

03Should I repair, refinish, or replace my bathtub?

It depends on the tub's condition and your goals. Minor cracks, stains or a dated color are often perfect for repair or refinishing. Structural damage, leaks or a full layout change usually call for replacement. We'll assess it honestly during your free estimate.
