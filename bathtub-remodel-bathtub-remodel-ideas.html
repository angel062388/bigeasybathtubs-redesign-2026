How to Remodel Your Bathtub

With our remodeling ideas, we can help you create the oasis of your dreams. Whether you’re looking for a modern or a traditional style, our team can provide solutions that will make your bathroom stand out from the rest. Let us show you how to transform your space into something special.

Replace the bathtub

Replacing the bathtub is one of the most important pieces of any successful bathtub remodel as it sets the tone for the entire room. A new bathtub will also bring a great return on your investment by increasing your home’s value and allowing you to enjoy your beautiful, renovated bathroom for years to come.

Install a new surround

If you’re looking for something more traditional but still want an updated look, then tile surrounds may be just what you need. Tile surrounds provide the perfect balance between modern and classic design styles while still offering plenty of customization options.

You can choose from various colors and patterns to create the perfect look for your bathroom – or even mix and match different tiles to create something truly unique!

Refinish the bathtub

The first step in any bathtub remodeling project is deciding whether you want to refinish or replace your existing tub. Refinishing is the process of repairing and restoring an existing tub instead of replacing it outright. This is usually done by applying a new layer of porcelain enamel over the existing material.

Refinishing typically costs less than replacement but may not be possible if there are underlying structural issues with the existing tub that need to be addressed.

Add a jetted tub

One ideal addition you can make is adding a jetted tub to your bathtub remodel. Jetted tubs are equipped with massaging jet systems that give off an indulgent swirling sensation as they provide relief to tired muscles and joints, offering the perfect spa retreat within your own home. Whether you prefer soaking or aromatherapy, jetted tubs offer a variety of health benefits that will upgrade any bathtub remodel.

Convert to a walk-in tub

You can also opt for walk-in models that make entering and exiting your tub easier than ever before.  Not only do walk-in tubs provide a great ratio between comfort and functionality, but they are also available with optional massage jets for a luxurious bathing experience. So if you’re looking for a hassle-free way to update your bathroom, make sure you consider converting to a walk-in tub!

Types of Bathtubs to Choose From

If you decide to go for the replacement, there are plenty of different types and styles of bathtubs available on the market today from Big Easy Bathtubs. Our selection includes classic clawfoot models as well as modern freestanding designs in various sizes and colors.

We also offer whirlpools and air baths for those who want the ultimate relaxation during their next soak! For larger bathrooms, we even have corner bathtubs that can fit two people at once for added convenience.

Freestanding Tubs

One of the most popular options these days is the freestanding tub. These tubs are perfect if you are looking for a more contemporary look, as they stand on their own without being attached to a wall. Not only do they look beautiful, but they also come in various sizes and materials, so there are plenty of options available when it comes to finding one that fits your style and budget.

Shower/Tub Combinations

If you don’t have enough room in your bathroom for both a shower and a bathtub, then consider combining them into one! A shower/tub combination is perfect for those who want the convenience of having both in one space without taking up too much room. Big Easy Bathtubs has plenty of options available in this style, ranging from classic clawfoot tubs with showers built-in to modern designs with sleek lines.

Cost of Bathtub Remodeling

Bathtub remodeling is often a sought-after home upgrade for both functionality and aesthetic purposes. There are many factors to consider when assessing bathtub remodeling costs such as bathtub type, size, and material of the bathtub itself.

Additional costs may vary depending on the details of the remodel, but typically bathtubs may range from $1,000 all the way to $10,000 or more. With careful planning and budgeting, bathtub remodeling can turn any bath into a luxurious oasis without breaking the bank.
