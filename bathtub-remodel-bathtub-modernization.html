Improve Your Bathtub’s Looks and Style With Modern Design Ideas in New Orleans, Louisiana

Are you planning to modernize a bathtub in New Orleans? There are many different ways to upgrade an old bathtub and add value to your home. With the right technique, you can modernize any old tub into the perfect design for your home.

The city of New Orleans is filled with historic homes, many with bathrooms that still feature their original bathtubs.

Even if you don’t plan on renovating your entire bathroom, improving the look of your bathtub can be a great way to add instant value to your house and express yourself through creative design.

Modernizing a bathtub in New Orleans is no small task, as there are many factors to consider, such as cost, materials, and installation techniques.

We will discuss the various options available to make it easier for anyone considering a bathtub update. Big Easy Bathtubs will talk about tips and tricks on how you can transform any outdated tub into an eye-catching centerpiece, no matter where you live in New Orleans.

Modern Ways to Improve the Look of Your Bathtub

Improving the look of your bathtub can be achieved in many modern ways. One way is to add a new coating of protective epoxy paint, which will help to prevent rust and damage. Consider trying different colors and patterns to create a unique look that will make a statement.

Installing high-end tile or stone around the tub, along with lighting accents, can create an inviting environment and make this feature of your bathroom stand out dramatically. If you’re feeling overwhelmed by your options, enlisting the assistance of a local interior designer can help you create your very own oasis right in your own home.

Solid-Colored Tubs

Solid-colored bathtubs have become increasingly popular in modern bathrooms as they provide a sleek and stylish look that cannot be achieved with other materials. The solid color also helps to brighten up a bathroom with its vibrancy and can complement existing décor or act as the focal point of your room.

Additionally, solid-colored bathtubs are easy to maintain and clean – all you need to do is give them a quick wipe-down regularly, and you can forget about long scrubbing sessions! Solid-colored tubs are an excellent way to update any bathroom while staying within budget.

Neutral Tub Surrounds

Neutral tub surrounds are the perfect way to give your bathtub a modern, low-maintenance look. With light materials, like natural stone and marble, neutral tub surrounds allow you to create a timeless look that won’t go out of style. To add warmth and texture, opt for stone elements or tiles in a range of subtle shades.

You can also choose to enhance the look with accessories like mirrors, art pieces, or even potted plants. By using neutral colors and tones for your tub surrounds, you create a classic spa-like atmosphere that will be both calming and stylish for years to come.

Reface the Side of the Bathtub With Decorative Elements

Refacing the side of the bathtub with decorative elements can be a modern and affordable way to improve the look of your bathtub. Depending on your budget, you can choose from a variety of materials, such as tile, marble, wood, or fiberglass.

Refacing can involve replacing existing panels with new, coordinated ones or simply adding some visual accents such as stripes, mosaics, or appliques. This type of renovation will give your bathroom an updated look and feel while also increasing its value.

It is much quicker and cheaper than replacing the entire tub which requires additional repairs to walls and floors. Therefore, refacing is truly an excellent way to upgrade your bathroom without having to make major changes.

Paint the Exterior Of Your Claw-Foot Tub

If you have a claw-foot tub, painting the exterior can be a great and relatively inexpensive way to give it an updated look.

There are several tips for choosing the right paint for your project:

  1. Choose acrylic paints that have been formulated specifically for bathrooms.
  2. Use a semi-gloss sheen for ease of cleaning.
  3. Give extra attention to surfaces under faucets and drains, so that water won’t affect the integrity of the paint job.

All you need is plenty of masking tape, fine-grit sandpaper, a quality drill brush, and multiple thin coats of your chosen sealant and paint. By following these tips when painting your claw-foot tub, you can achieve a beautiful look in no time.

Put New Tiling Around Your Tub

Replacing the tiling around your tub is a great way to improve its look and give it an instant facelift. Tiles come in a variety of colors, styles, sizes, and materials so you can create a unique design to suit your taste.

You can also incorporate different patterns, mosaics, or glass designs which will help add depth and detail to the look of the tub.

Additionally, updating the tub’s fixtures such as showerheads, handles and faucets will help enhance the overall appearance of the space. Installing new tiling around your tub is an effective way to instantly elevate your bathroom’s aesthetic without breaking the bank.

Invest In A High-Quality Bathtub Resurfacing

Investing in high-quality bathtub resurfacing is one of the most cost-effective ways to update the look of your bathroom.

Bathtub resurfacing can instantly refresh and make the bathtub look brand new again. This process also increases the longevity of the tub and prevents any further cracks, chips, or damage from occurring. There are different types of bathtub refinishing treatments available for whatever you choose that best suits your needs and budget.

Professional installation services can help you get great results with minimal effort. Investing in high-quality bathtub resurfacing is a great way to improve both the aesthetics and value of your home.
