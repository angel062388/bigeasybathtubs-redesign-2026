New Orleans, Louisiana’s Senior Friendly Bathtubs For Your Loved One’s Safety and Mobility

As the aging population continues to grow, people over 65 now make up about 16 percent of the total U.S. population. This means that there is an increasing demand for products and services designed specifically for seniors. Bathroom remodeling can be a daunting task to take on. It’s important for seniors in particular to consider their safety, comfort, and accessibility when doing renovations.

Luckily, there are now a number of professional companies that specialize in assisting senior citizens with bathtub renovations, like Big Easy Bathtubs. We will discuss how seniors in New Orleans can benefit from bathtub remodeling services and offer tips on making your project successful.

Tips for Remodeling a Bathroom Especially Made for Aging Seniors

When remodeling a bathroom specially designed for an aging senior in New Orleans, it is important to consider safety first.

Choose a Bathroom Layout That Promotes Safety and Prevents Falls

When remodeling a bathroom for an aging senior in New Orleans, it is important to think about the safety and prevention of falls. In this case, the ideal layout plan should consider the need for space to maneuver a wheelchair or walker and install grab bars near the toilet and bathtub areas.

Make Sure the Bathroom Has Lots of Space and Proper Storage to Easily Access Items

When remodeling a bathroom specifically designed for an aging senior in New Orleans, one should consider making sure there is plenty of space and storage to access items easily. One can add additional cabinets, shelves, and drawers to create storage for items like towels, bathrobes, and toiletries.

Invest in Thoughtful Details Such as Higher Toilets and Lever Handles on Doors, and Faucets to Make Them Easier to Use

When remodeling a bathroom for an aging senior in New Orleans, thoughtful details should be taken into consideration. Higher toilets, lever handles on doors, and faucets should be installed, as they make using them much easier.

Install Non-slip Surfaces, Strategically Placed Grab Bars, and Hand Shower on a Hose for Bathing Flexibility

When remodeling a bathroom specially designed for an aging senior in New Orleans, it is important to consider several elements of accessibility and safety. Non-slip surfaces should be installed on the floor and walls to reduce injuries from slipping or falling.

How Much Does Bathroom Remodeling Specifically For Seniors Usually Cost?

The cost of a bathroom remodel specifically for seniors depends on the size and complexity of the project. Generally, a minor renovation such as replacing fixtures, retiling floors, and updating lighting can cost between $5,000 and $10,000, while more extensive projects, including adding additional space or changing the layout, may cost significantly more, up to around $25,000 or more.

How To Make Sure A Bathroom is Designed Safe For Seniors?

When designing a bathroom for seniors, safety should be the top priority. First, the flooring needs to have non-slip features such as textured tiles or rubber treads that lead to the bathtub and shower area.

Add Grab Bars

Installing grab bars in a bathroom designed for seniors is essential to making sure the space is accessible and safe. Grab bars are a specific type of bar that can be mounted near a toilet, shower, bathtub, or other surfaces in the bathroom.

Choose Flat Surfaces With Good Grip

When designing a bathroom for seniors, making sure it is safe and functional should be the top priority. One way to do this is to choose flat surfaces that provide a good grip. Bathroom floors should be non-slip in order to decrease the risk of falls.

Install a Walk-in Shower or Tub With a Non-skid Bottom and Side Rails

Installing a walk-in shower or tub with a non-skid bottom and side rails is essential for designing a bathroom that is safe for seniors. Non-skid flooring can prevent falls, which are the most common injuries found in baths.

Place the Toilet Higher Than Usual So That It Is Easier to Get Up and Down

It is important to design a senior-friendly bathroom in order to maintain safety and reduce the risk of falls. The toilet should be placed higher than usual, as it would be easier for seniors to get up and down when using it. An armrest can also be included beside the toilet for added support.

Use a Chair With Arms to Help Them Sit Down and Stand Up Safely

Using a chair with arms in the design of senior-friendly bathrooms can help seniors safely sit down and stand up. When planning the design of the bathroom, consider chairs or benches with armrests to add support when getting on and off the toilet or stepping into the bathtub. This type of seating will also add stability for those who may be unsteady on their feet.

Also, make sure there is sufficient lighting around toilets and tubs to ensure that seniors can see clearly while they use the facilities. Finally, it is important to choose slip-resistant floors around the toilet and shower area to prevent falls while getting in and out of the bathtub.
