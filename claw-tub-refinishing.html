01How Long Does it Take to Refinish a Clawfoot Tub?

If you try to complete a refinishing yourself, it might take you anywhere between a few hours to several days, especially if you’re not experienced with refinishing tubs.

Our professional contractors will be able to get the job done in as little as three to five hours, depending on your bathtub’s condition. Should you have an old tub that’s damaged, it may need special attention before the reglazing can be applied.

In normal conditions, an tub refinishing can take less than 24 hours to become fully dry and ready for use. If it needs a full reglaze, the curing time will be a little longer.

The speed of the process will also depend on the kind of coating you choose for the bathtub reglazing. When the drying process is over, you’ll have a beautiful clawfoot bathtub that you can enjoy for years to come.

02How Much Does it Cost to Refinish a Clawfoot Tub?

Don’t replace your bathtub right away — refinishing your bathtub in New Orleans will save you hundreds of dollars and is the best option for those on a budget. While a new bathtub may cost as little as $300, there are often hidden charges that come along with them.

When you add the plumbing modifications, removal and disposal charges, as well as tile work to the bill, you can easily fall into a trap, paying as much as $2,500 for everything.

At Big Easy Bathtubs, we offer transparent pricing from day one and we’ll be sure to update you on any changes that may occur during the project.

Furthermore, we can work on the entire bathroom, rather than just providing you with a replacement bathtub. With our team, you’ll save on both time and money, where we’ll have everything done in as little as 24 hours and save you as much as 75 to 90 percent of the cost of replacing your tub!

03What is bathtub refinishing, and how long does it last?

Refinishing restores the surface of your existing tub. We repair chips and stains and apply a fresh, durable coating for a like-new look at a fraction of the cost of replacement. With proper care, a quality refinish typically lasts 10 to 15 years.

04Should I repair, refinish, or replace my bathtub?

It depends on the tub's condition and your goals. Minor cracks, stains or a dated color are often perfect for repair or refinishing. Structural damage, leaks or a full layout change usually call for replacement. We'll assess it honestly during your free estimate.

05Do you restore antique clawfoot tubs?

Absolutely. We refinish and restore clawfoot and other vintage tubs, inside and out, bringing back their classic charm while making them fully functional for everyday use.
