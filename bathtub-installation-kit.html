1. What’s Included in the Kit

Our bathtub installation kit includes everything you need for a successful bathtub installation. The kit comes with a detailed instruction manual to guide you through the process, as well as all the necessary components for a successful installation.

This includes a drain assembly, supply lines, mounting hardware, and more. In addition, our kits come with all the materials and tools you need to complete the job – so you will not need to buy any extra items.

2. How to Assemble the Kit

The installation of our kits is simple and stress-free. In addition, each kit comes with an easy-to-follow instruction manual that will walk you through the entire process from start to finish!

Furthermore, our kits are crafted to be user-friendly so that even if you never installed a tub before, you could still achieve the desired results with ease.

3. Step-by-Step Installation Instructions

Installing a bathtub can be as effortless and stress-free as ever with the Big Easy Bathtubs installation kit. To get started, make sure to thoroughly read the accompanying instruction manual that comes with your kit. This manual serves as your go-to guide for putting together and fitting in your fresh bathtub, offering step-by-step instructions that you can easily follow.

  • To begin the installation process, it’s essential to ready the space where you’ll be adding your bathtub.

This job entails defining the area, delineating walls and flooring, as well as making sure all essential plumbing systems are properly installed. Accurately measuring each component is an integral part of this process.

  • Once you have prepped the space, it’s time to start putting together your bathtub. Just follow the instructions in the manual and everything should be a breeze!

This includes attaching the drain assembly, supply lines, mounting hardware, and any other components included in your kit.

  • Finally, once everything is assembled correctly, you can begin the installation process.

This includes securing the bathtub to the walls and floor, connecting all necessary plumbing connections, and testing for leaks.

Congrats! You have successfully finished the installation, and now your new bathtub is all set to be used.

4. Troubleshooting Tips

When installing a bathtub, troubleshooting any difficulties you may come across is essential for success. If something isn’t going as planned, taking a breath and diagnosing the issue can save time in the long run and help ensure your installation goes off without a hitch.

When attempting to solve any issues, your first step should be consulting the instruction manual that came with your kit. This will provide you with precise instructions on how best to assemble and install your bathtub correctly.

If you are still having trouble, you can also contact our customer service team for assistance. Our team is available to answer any questions or provide additional guidance on how to complete your installation successfully.

5. Safety Precautions

When it comes to installing a bathtub, safety should never be compromised – take measures beforehand to ensure everyone’s well-being. Put on protective equipment such as clothing and eyewear; additionally, handle tools with extreme caution and keep materials away from areas where they could cause injury or damage.

Taking these precautions will help guarantee that the installation process goes smoothly without any hiccups along the way!

Additionally, it is important to make sure that all electrical connections are properly grounded and that any gas lines are properly sealed. Finally, be sure to follow all instructions provided in the instruction manual to ensure a safe and successful installation.

6. Tools Needed for Installation

To successfully install your bathtub with our installation kit, you will need several essential tools: a drill, screwdriver, adjustable wrench, level, and tape measure. Depending on the type of tub you are installing though, there may be other pieces of equipment necessary as well.

For example, if you are installing a drop-in tub, you will need to have access to a jigsaw or reciprocating saw.

7. Care and Maintenance Guide

Proper care and maintenance of your bathtub are essential to ensure its longevity. To keep your bathtub looking its best, it is important to clean it regularly with mild soap and warm water.

Furthermore, make sure to thoroughly examine the tub for any wear and tear like chips or cracks. If noticed, it is important to repair them as soon as possible in order to prevent further damage from occurring.

Lastly, always check all plumbing connections regularly for potential leaks or other complications that may arise.
