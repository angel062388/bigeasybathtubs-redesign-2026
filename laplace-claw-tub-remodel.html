Clawfoot Bathtub Remodel in LaPlace

Your bathroom is one of the most important rooms in your house. It’s where you start and end your day. So, why not make it a place where you can relax and rejuvenate in?

If you have old bathtubs or shower enclosures, it may be time for a change. If you’re in LaPlace area, Big Easy Bathtubs can transform your bathroom with a beautiful clawfoot bathtub remodel.

Advantages of Claw Tub to Shower Remodeling in LaPlace

A clawfoot bathtub is a great way to add character and charm to your bathroom. They are not only stylish but also quite functional. Adding showers to your current setup, however, can give you the best of both worlds.

Three Reasons for Claw Tub to Shower Remodeling:

  1. 01

    Beauty

    Clawfoot tubs have a classic, timeless beauty that is unmatched by any other type of tub on the market. When you remodel your bathroom and add a shower to your clawfoot tub, you are sure to increase the value of your home.

    Clawfoot tubs have that certain wow factor. They are uniquely beautiful with their ornate feet and curving lines. They make a statement in any bathroom. They also complement the home style that you have, such as traditional, Victorian, or even contemporary.

    Whether or not you’re planning to sell your home in the near future, a clawfoot tub and shower is always a good investment, especially for the master bedroom’s ensuite. You can find clawfoot tubs in a variety of materials, such as cast iron, porcelain-coated steel, acrylic, and more.

    No matter what your budget is, you’re sure to find the perfect clawfoot tub and shower combination for your home with us.

  2. 02

    Convenience

    Having both a tub and shower in one unit is very convenient. It means that you can have a shower when you want one and relax in a tub when you want to. Not only will you enjoy soaking in your new tub but you can also take quick showers whenever you’re in a rush.

    Another great thing about clawfoot tubs is that they’re usually much deeper than regular tubs. This means that you can relax and soak in the tub without having to worry about your feet sticking out. This way, you can soak all your worries away and come out feeling refreshed and rejuvenated.

    You can unwind after a long day at work in your clawfoot bathtub and then take a quick shower the next morning without having to compromise on efficiency and convenience.

  3. 03

    Savings

    You can also save time, energy, and space in your bathroom when you have a clawfoot tub to shower remodel. When you take a shower, all of the water stays in the tub. This means that you do not have to worry about spilling water on the floor and making a mess.

    If you have a small bathroom, you may want to consider converting your clawfoot bathtub into a shower to get the best of both worlds. You don’t have to worry about taking up too much space in your bathroom when you have a clawfoot tub.

    You’ll be able to save money on your water bill each month when you have a clawfoot tub. This is because you will not be using as much water when you take showers. You’ll also be able to save money on your energy bill because you will not have to heat as much water with this type of bathroom setup.

About LaPlace

Getting to know LaPlace, Louisiana

LaPlace is in St. John the Baptist Parish, on the east bank of the Mississippi River about 25 miles from New Orleans.

  1. Things to Do

    Visit the 1811 Kid Ory Historic House, or come in October for the Andouille Festival, held every year since 1972.

  2. Nearby Suburbs

    Reserve is close by. Our team also works in St. Rose and Kenner, between LaPlace and New Orleans.

  3. Fun Facts

    A Louisiana governor named LaPlace the “Andouille Capital of the World”. The town takes its name from Basile Laplace, after the railroad depot was named for him in 1883.

  4. Public Transportation

    The River Parishes Transit Authority, based in LaPlace, runs a curb-to-curb ride service booked a day ahead. Interstates 10 and 55 both pass through.

02How Much Does It Cost To Remodel a Clawfoot Tub?

Clawfoot bathtub remodels are a popular home improvement project in New Orleans. The average cost to remodel a clawfoot tub is $2,500, but can range from $1,000 to $4,000 depending on the size and condition of your tub, as well as the type of fixtures you choose.

Additional fixtures, such as a shower, can add to the costs of your clawfoot tub remodel. Shower prices can range from $500 to $5,000, while faucets can cost anywhere from $100 to $1,000.

Other possible installations can be a rainfall showerhead, which can cost around $200, or a hand shower, which can cost between $50 and $500. If you have an existing bathroom that will require significant plumbing work to accommodate your new tub, those costs could add up as well.

The remodeling cost may seem a bit hefty upfront, but you have to consider the long-term savings that you can enjoy. Clawfoot tubs are known to last for generations, so this is an investment that will certainly pay off in the long run.

When you remodel your clawfoot tub, you can choose from a wide range of fixtures, accessories, and materials. For instance, you can opt for a cast iron clawfoot tub which is extremely durable and can last a lifetime. Or, you may want to go for a more modern look with a clawfoot tub made from acrylic.

No matter what style you choose, your clawfoot bathtub remodel is sure to add value and charm to your home.

03Should I repair, refinish, or replace my bathtub?

It depends on the tub's condition and your goals. Minor cracks, stains or a dated color are often perfect for repair or refinishing. Structural damage, leaks or a full layout change usually call for replacement. We'll assess it honestly during your free estimate.

04Do you restore antique clawfoot tubs?

Absolutely. We refinish and restore clawfoot and other vintage tubs, inside and out, bringing back their classic charm while making them fully functional for everyday use.
