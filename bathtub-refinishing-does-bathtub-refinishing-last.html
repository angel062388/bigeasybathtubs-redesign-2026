Discovering the Durability of Bathtub Refinishing: How Long Does it Last?

Your bathroom is looking a bit drab, and you’re considering giving it a new look. You might be considering bathtub refinishing as an option, but how long does it actually last?

Refinishing your bathtub can provide an affordable way to make a significant difference in the appearance of your bathroom. But you may have concerns about how well it will work and how long it will last.

Bathtub refinishing by Big Easy Bathtubs is a great way to give your bathroom an extreme makeover without breaking the bank. It’s also important to understand that this process may not last forever, depending on the quality of the materials and the care taken during installation.

In this article, we’ll explore the longevity of bathtub refinishing and when or if you should consider getting it redone.

The Durability of Bathtub Refinishing

Bathtub refinishing is a great way to make your old bathtub look new again in just a few hours. Not only does bathtub refinishing provide a beautiful finish to an outdated tub, but it’s also incredibly durable. With proper care, the finish can last for several years, making it a wise choice for those looking for an attractive and long-lasting bathtub surface.

The bathtub refinishing process involves the application of a special coating that bonds with the old bathtub’s existing porcelain or enamel to create a smooth and glossy finish that is easy to maintain and clean. This makes bathtub refinishing not only affordable and convenient but also highly durable.

Types of Bathtub Refinishing and their Durability

Bathtub refinishing is becoming increasingly popular, as it is a great way to instantly upgrade the look of your bathroom.

There are various types of refinishing and each one has different levels of durability. In this article, we’ll cover the different types and how long they last.

Standard Refinishing

Standard tub refinishing is the most cost-effective option for homeowners and often includes cleaning, sanding, and preparing the surface before painting on a topcoat. The topcoat will usually be either epoxy or acrylic.

The epoxy gives a little bit of protection against wear and tear, but it is not very strong. Generally, standard bathroom refinished tubs have a lifespan of around 5–7 years.

Acrylic Refinishing

Acrylic finish makes use of a polyurethane coating which provides good protection against wear and tear on the surface area it covers. This type of bathroom refinishes usually lasts 8–10 years before showing signs of fading or discoloration.

Acrylic finish doesn’t require any additional preparation like sanding before applying the polyurethane enamel as all that’s needed is a clean surface for application.

Fiberglass Refinishing

Fiberglass refinishing takes a slightly different approach since fiberglass tubs can not easily be stripped down to their base material like regular tub repair work can be. Instead, only repairs are done; then special adhesives are used for securing them followed by specifically formulated primers and topcoats to give the finish a smooth, consistent coat that lasts for a long time and does not break easily.

It also comes with a longer lifespan than standard porcelain tubs where they last up to 10-15 years without needing any additional maintenance work during this time frame, saving you both time and money as well!

Factors that Affect the Longevity of Bathtub Refinishing

The lifespan of a tub refinishing project can depend on many different factors. It’s important to take these into account when you’re deciding how to best invest in your bathroom remodeling.

Here’s what you need to know about the factors that can extend or reduce the longevity of a bathtub refinishing job:

Proper Preparation Before Refinishing

One of the most important steps for a successful and long-lasting bathtub refinishing project is proper preparation. This includes cleaning and prepping the surface, such as power washing it and making sure all soap scum, dirt, and debris are completely removed.

Additionally, any existing rust should be sanded down before applying fresh paint or resin to ensure a level and smooth surface that won’t bubble or crack down the road.

Use Quality Products and Materials

Using quality products and materials is essential in order to make sure that your refinishing job will last longer. Opting for professional-grade paints, primers, sealants, tapes, caulking agents, etc., will cost more, but they are often worth it compared to regular products available at home improvement stores.

Careful Application of Coatings

In order for coatings to be effective in maintaining a healthy finish, they must be applied properly from start to finish and not chip away or wear quickly after just a couple of months. In other words, “just trust the process” by consulting with professional refinishers ahead of time – who can help determine which type of coating works best for your budget!

If not done correctly from the get-go, DIY attempts can lead to expensive damage.

Regular Maintenance

When it comes to bathtub maintenance, it’s also important to be proactive; that means regularly wiping down surfaces with an antiseptic cleaner or diluted vinegar mixture every couple weeks – as well as scrubbing off any visible mold growth – this will eliminate bacteria accumulation over time.

Keeping an eye out for chipped areas is highly recommended too, so these can be filled right away before it becomes worse.

Proper Maintenance to Extend the Life of Bathtub Refinishing

Bathtub refinishing is a great way to give your bathroom a fresh, new look without the expense and hassle of replacing the entire tub. But how long does refinished bathtub last? The answer depends on several factors, including the type of refinishing used, the quality of materials used, and proper maintenance.

To extend the life of bathtub refinishing, you can do the following:

  1. Clear any debris or particles from the surface regularly.
  2. Use non-abrasive, mild detergents to clean the tub.
  3. Avoid services that cause impact, like drilling on the bathtub refinishing.
  4. Take additional steps to minimize moisture in the room such as ventilation and open windows.
