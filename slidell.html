About Slidell

Getting to know Slidell, Louisiana

Slidell is in St. Tammany Parish, on the northeast shore of Lake Pontchartrain.

  1. Things to Do

    Browse the shops and restaurants of Olde Towne Slidell, visit the Slidell Museum, or launch a boat from Heritage Park.

  2. Nearby Suburbs

    Pearl River and Pearlington, Mississippi are close by. Our team also works in Mandeville and Covington.

  3. Fun Facts

    Slidell grew up in 1882 and 1883 around the building of the New Orleans and Northeastern Railroad. It is named for the politician John Slidell.

  4. Public Transportation

    Amtrak’s Crescent stops at Slidell station, and Interstates 10, 12 and 59 meet here. St. Tammany Parish runs STAR Transit, a curb-to-curb ride service booked by phone ahead of time.
