FAQs

Bathtub questions, answered

Still have questions? We're happy to help.

View all FAQs
01How long does a bathtub installation take?

Most standard bathtub installations are completed in one to two days. The timeline can vary with the type of tub, any plumbing changes, and the condition of your existing bathroom. We'll give you a clear estimate before we begin.

02Do you install walk-in tubs for seniors and accessibility?

Yes. Walk-in tubs are one of our specialties. We help you choose a safe, comfortable model with features like a low step-in threshold, built-in seating and grab bars, then handle the complete installation.

03What is bathtub refinishing, and how long does it last?

Refinishing restores the surface of your existing tub. We repair chips and stains and apply a fresh, durable coating for a like-new look at a fraction of the cost of replacement. With proper care, a quality refinish typically lasts 10 to 15 years.

04Should I repair, refinish, or replace my bathtub?

It depends on the tub's condition and your goals. Minor cracks, stains or a dated color are often perfect for repair or refinishing. Structural damage, leaks or a full layout change usually call for replacement. We'll assess it honestly during your free estimate.

05Do you restore antique clawfoot tubs?

Absolutely. We refinish and restore clawfoot and other vintage tubs, inside and out, bringing back their classic charm while making them fully functional for everyday use.
