Bathtub Installation Types for New Orleans Homes

Investing in a quality bathtub is key if you’re looking to upgrade your bathroom to a source of ultimate relaxation. But how do you determine which type of bathtub installation would be best for your New Orleans home? You’ll want a tub that will look great in the space and function well for years to come.

Big Easy Bathtubs offers an array of high-quality bath installations tailored specifically to meet Louisiana homeowners’ needs, including soaker tubs, whirlpool baths, combination baths, and more. Read on to learn about our different types of installations and why they are perfect for bathrooms throughout New Orleans!

What Are the Different Types of Bathtub Installation?

The types of bathtub installation can vary greatly depending on the type of bathtub you have, its location, and what type of hookups are available to you. The most common types include alcoves, drop-in/platform, freestanding, corner, and soaking tubs.

All types of installation will require some form of anchoring – either mounted directly to the wall studs with screws or when installing into tile/stone surfaces – use specially formulated cement adhesive.

Alcove

Alcove bathtubs are popular among homeowners because they are easy to install and provide a timeless, classic look. Alcove bathtubs come pre-assembled, making installation relatively straightforward.

To properly install the Alcove bathtub, there should be enough space in the bathroom, and ensure that the walls are strong and level enough to support it. Also, if the Alcove bathtub is being placed on the floor or exists between two walls that are not level then shims may have to be used.

After this is complete, waterlines are hooked up securely so that the Alcove bathtub will run nice and smoothly for years to come!

Drop-in

Drop-in bathtubs offer a stunning addition to any bathroom. These models are installed over an opening on your floor, creating a unique look with outstanding style and design. Drop-in bathtubs come in a variety of shapes and sizes, so you can find one that perfectly suits your individual needs.

During installation, the bathtub is sunk into the surface of the flooring material and sealed around the edges to ensure there is no water leakage. Drop-in tubs provide an elegant touch to any lavatory, offering timeless beauty and an unmistakable feeling of luxury.

With proper monitoring and maintenance, drop-in bathtubs are designed to last for years – providing you with peaceful relaxation during every soothing soak.

Freestanding

Freestanding bathtubs add a touch of style and luxury to any bathroom, but their installation can be complicated. Freestanding tubs must be set onto a dedicated platform or surrounded by tiled surfaces, and in some cases, extra supports may need to be added for extra stability.

Our experienced plumbers are best equipped to install a freestanding bathtub safely and securely, making sure it works perfectly with the existing layout of your bathroom. With the right approach from our team, enjoying a luxurious soak in your new bathtub can be yours in no time!

What Are the Benefits and Drawbacks of Each Type of Installation?

When deciding how to install a bathtub, it is important to consider the pros and cons of each option. There are three main types: Drop-in, Alcove, and Freestanding.

  • A Drop-in bathtub can be customized for length, width, shape, and material but can be difficult to clean as it does not have a self-contained skirt or ledge.
  • An Alcove bathtub is installed within an alcove formed by three walls which is great for conserving space without sacrificing comfort, however, the addition of custom fixtures makes it more costly than other options.
  • Freestanding bathtubs are great if you have a larger space but they typically require stability bolts which must be drilled into the flooring beneath.

Each type of installation has its advantages and disadvantages to consider when making your decision.

How Do I Know Which Type of Bathtub Installation Is Right for My Home?

When it comes to choosing the right type of bathtub installation for your home, there are a few factors to consider.

  1. The size of your bathroom should be taken into account as this is a key part of determining which type of installation will fit in the space appropriately.
  2. You need to consider the costs involved as installations can vary widely in price depending on materials used and level of difficulty.
  3. Be sure to always consult a professional before going ahead with any installation decisions such as whether or not to build out or replace walls, or figure out how much water pressure and drainage is needed.

All this knowledge together will help you make an informed decision on which bathtub installation is right for your home.

Common Mistakes to Avoid During Installation

Installing a bathtub in your residential home is no small task, and it’s important to make sure it’s done correctly. Otherwise, you could run into costly repairs and inconvenience down the road. When attempting bathtub installation, some of the most common mistakes you should avoid are the following:

  • Include purchasing components that aren’t compatible with each other,
  • Using incorrect methods for plumbing drain connections,
  • Failing to check for level when installing the bathtub,
  • Not sealing around the bathtub correctly, and
  • Working without a plan or guide.

Taking time for proper preparation and research will ensure your bathtub installation goes as smoothly as possible.
