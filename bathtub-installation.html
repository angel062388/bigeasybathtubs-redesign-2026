Why Would You Want To Install a New Tub?

Installing a new bathtub is more beneficial than you think.

  • First, it gives you unparalleled comfort.
  • Since you won’t have to worry about stubborn stains, scratches, leaks, and other problems, you can enjoy your own quiet time in the tub more and relax better.
  • You can also opt for a bigger, more modern, or more ergonomic design. Thus, you can elevate the aesthetics of your space and are free to change things up like the tiles and other decors around your tub.
  • Having a fresh bathtub in your bathroom can also help increase your house’s value and marketability. It offers a family-friendly environment, given that tubs make it easier for young children to take a bath.

If you install a new bathtub, you can experience better health, too. This includes easing muscle pain, managing inflammation, and improving circulation. Hot baths are also associated with better heart function and lower blood pressure.

Choosing a new tub, like a walk-in or roll-in design, makes bathing safer and more accessible as you don’t have to get in and out of the tub or step over a barrier.

What To Consider Before Installing a New Tub?

Planning to install a new bathtub in your New Orleans home?

Here are the things you should consider before proceeding with the new bathtub installation:

1. Bathroom Space

The style and type of bathtub you’ll install in your home depend on the layout and size of your bathroom. For instance, if you have a small bathroom and you want to maximize space, you can choose the drop-in or corner tub or the alcove/shower combination.

Bathtubs with a standard size usually occupy a floor space of 13 square feet. However, you can choose bigger bathtub options based on the space, your needs, and your preferences.

2. Materials

Installing the best tub in your New Orleans home also requires deciding on the core materials. For instance, do you prefer fiberglass or acrylic which makes the interior hollow and reduces weight? Do you want heavier tubs that utilize enamel and metal? Other modern yet more expensive tubs are made of natural stone. Sturdier materials have a higher capacity to prevent damage.

3. Cost and Quality

Choose trusted companies to experience top-notch bathtub installation in New Orleans. Moreover, high-quality products such as bathtubs offer a limited lifetime warranty. Before starting the project with a bathroom remodeling company, get free installation tub costs to estimate first. To achieve a balance in cost and quality, pick a bathtub that’s affordable yet durable.

Bathtub Features

If you want the best bathing experience, consider various bathtub features, like the following:

1. Chromatherapy

Chromatherapy tubs include underwater LED lights. These will change color to complement your mood. For example, you have blue for a more soothing feel, pink for peace, and more.

2. Jets and Bubbles

Whirlpool tubs have various speed settings that control the jets and bubbles’ vigor. There are also in-line heaters to warm water while circulating, preventing heat loss. Silent motor technology helps decrease the jets’ sound. Then, foot massage jets are placed on top of submerged foot support.

3. Music

Some tubs include resonant speakers that play music and offer underwater vibrations on your skin to calm you during bath time. You can sync it with your playlists on the computer.

4. Digital Controls

You can do everything from adjusting the temperature and controlling the jet flow and direction to changing the color and playing selected music with a built-in touchpad.

Regardless of the features you’re aiming for, we are here to give you superior New Orleans bathtub installation, affordable pricing, and durable bathroom products.

Bathtub Installation Cost in New Orleans

How much is the bathtub installation?

Installing or replacing a bathtub ranges from $4,360 to $7,290. However, the bathtub installation cost varies according to several factors. These include your chosen bathtub type and style.

For example, fiberglass and acrylic materials are more economical choices for tub replacement. You may also go for cast iron, which is more expensive and heavier.

Then, if you prefer a more luxurious design, you can choose a bathtub with stone material. Other factors include the size, additional fixtures, old tub removal, preparation of the surface, labor costs, permits required in New Orleans, and more.

Should I Install My Own Bathtub?

While you may want to make your bathtub installation a DIY project, this can waste your time, money, and other resources. For instance, improper installation can cause unlevel tub and water damage. From removing flooring and walls to placing pipes, connecting drain and water supply, and other complex details, you’ll need expert bathtub installers to perform the job well.

Moreover, you can skip the hassle of following every detail to make the installation process. You can also make sure that your home remains safe from any potential damage.
