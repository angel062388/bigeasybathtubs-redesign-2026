Enhance Your Bathing Experience: Accessories and Upgrades for Clawfoot Tubs

Clawfoot tubs are timeless and elegant, adding a touch of luxury to any bathroom. However, sometimes they need a little extra love and attention to truly make them shine. That’s where accessories and upgrades come in.

From stylish fixtures and faucets to practical add-ons like showerheads and caddies, there are many ways to enhance your clawfoot tub and make your bathing experience even more enjoyable.

At Big Easy Bathtubs, we offer a wide selection of accessories and upgrades designed specifically for clawfoot tubs. In this article, we’ll explore some of our top picks and show you how you can transform your tub into a luxurious oasis.

Fixtures and Faucets: Choosing the Right Style for Your Clawfoot Tub

When it comes to choosing fixtures and faucets for your clawfoot tub, it’s important to consider both functionality and style. While you want your fixtures and faucets to work well and last for years, you also want them to complement the overall look and feel of your bathroom.

One popular style for clawfoot tubs is the classic Victorian look, which typically includes intricate detailing and ornate handles. These fixtures and faucets often come in polished brass or chrome, which can give your bathroom a luxurious, vintage feel.

If you’re looking for something more modern, however, there are plenty of options available as well. Sleek, minimalist designs in brushed nickel or matte black can add a contemporary touch to your bathroom while still working well with the traditional look of a clawfoot tub.

When selecting your fixtures and faucets, also consider the type of tub you have. Some clawfoot tubs have holes drilled into the rim for the installation of the faucet, while others require a freestanding faucet. Be sure to measure your tub and consult with a professional if you’re unsure about which type of fixture or faucet is best for your specific tub.

In addition to style and functionality, you’ll also want to consider the quality of the materials used in your fixtures and faucets. Look for high-quality materials like brass or stainless steel that will withstand the test of time and resist rust and corrosion.

Showerheads and Conversion Kits

If you’re looking to add functionality to your clawfoot tub, a showerhead and conversion kit can be a great addition. This allows you to enjoy the convenience of a shower without having to sacrifice the vintage charm of your clawfoot tub.

When selecting a showerhead and conversion kit, it’s important to consider both the style and functionality. For example, a hand-held showerhead can provide added flexibility and convenience, allowing you to easily rinse off and clean your tub. On the other hand, a fixed showerhead can provide a more traditional look and feel.

Another important factor to consider is the type of conversion kit you need. Some clawfoot tubs come with pre-drilled holes for the installation of a showerhead, while others may require a separate conversion kit to be installed. Be sure to consult with a professional if you’re unsure about which type of conversion kit is best for your specific tub.

When it comes to style, there are many different options available for showerheads and conversion kits. From vintage-inspired designs with intricate details to sleek and modern styles, there’s sure to be a showerhead and conversion kit that perfectly complements the overall look and feel of your bathroom.

Storage and Organization: Caddies and Shelves for Clawfoot Tubs

Clawfoot tubs are often the centerpiece of a bathroom, but they can also take up a lot of space. This can make storage and organization a challenge, especially if you have limited bathroom storage options.

Fortunately, there are many caddies and shelves designed specifically for clawfoot tubs that can help you maximize your space and keep your bathroom organized.

One popular option is a caddy that fits over the rim of your clawfoot tub. This can provide a convenient and easily accessible storage option for your shampoo, soap, and other bath essentials. Look for caddies that are adjustable and designed to fit a variety of clawfoot tub sizes.

Another option is a shelf that attaches to the wall above your clawfoot tub. This can provide additional storage space while also adding to the overall aesthetic of your bathroom. Look for shelves made from durable materials like metal or glass, and choose a style that complements the look and feel of your bathroom.

If you’re short on space, consider a corner caddy that fits snugly into the corner of your bathroom. This can provide additional storage without taking up valuable floor space. Look for corner caddies that are adjustable and designed to fit a variety of clawfoot tub sizes.

Lighting: Illuminating Your Tub and Creating a Relaxing Atmosphere

Lighting is an important element in any bathroom design, and it can be particularly impactful in creating a relaxing atmosphere for your clawfoot tub.

Here are some tips for illuminating your tub and creating a cozy and inviting environment:

  • Consider adding a dimmer switch to your bathroom lighting. This can allow you to adjust the lighting to create a softer and more relaxing atmosphere when you’re using your clawfoot tub.
  • Install a fixture directly above your clawfoot tub to provide direct lighting. A pendant light or chandelier can add a touch of elegance and sophistication to your bathroom while providing ample lighting for your tub.
  • Use wall sconces to provide additional lighting and create a cozy and inviting atmosphere. Wall sconces can be placed on either side of your clawfoot tub to create a balanced and symmetrical look.
  • Consider adding candles to your bathroom decor. Candles can provide soft and warm lighting that is perfect for a relaxing soak in your clawfoot tub. Look for scented candles with relaxing fragrances like lavender or eucalyptus to enhance the overall atmosphere of your bathroom.
  • Choose lighting fixtures that complement the overall style and aesthetic of your bathroom. From vintage-inspired designs to modern and minimalist styles, there are many different lighting options available that can perfectly complement your clawfoot tub and create the ambiance you’re looking for.

Clawfoot Tub Feet Replacing and Upgrading for a Fresh Look

Here are some tips for replacing and upgrading your clawfoot tub feet:

  • Start by assessing the current condition of your clawfoot tub feet. If they are damaged or rusted, it may be time to replace them entirely. Alternatively, if you simply want to update the look of your tub, you can consider upgrading to new feet that better complement your bathroom decor.
  • When selecting new feet for your clawfoot tub, consider the overall style and aesthetic of your bathroom. From traditional and ornate designs to modern and minimalist styles, there are many different options available that can perfectly complement your bathroom decor.
  • Choose feet that are made from durable materials like cast iron or brass to ensure long-lasting durability and performance.
  • Consider adding a fresh coat of paint to your clawfoot tub feet to give them a new and updated look. Choose a paint color that complements the overall color scheme of your bathroom and that is formulated for use on metal surfaces.
  • If you’re not comfortable replacing your clawfoot tub feet on your own, consider consulting with a professional plumber or contractor who can help you choose and install the right feet for your tub.
